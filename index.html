Any combination of these patches is safe: none of them overlap. If two selected patches ever write different bytes to the same place, the patcher refuses and names them. The English translation is applied first and the gameplay patches go on top of it. A ROM bigger than 16 MiB is padded to the full 32 MiB. The .bps files also work in Flips or Rom Patcher JS, but only one at a time: a gameplay .bps there needs the plain English ROM. To combine patches, use this page. Most mods are by the Chinese SS2 modding community.
